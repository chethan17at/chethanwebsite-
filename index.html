About the Series

Seven Kingdoms.
One Iron Throne.

Based on George R.R. Martin's bestselling series A Song of Ice and Fire, Game of Thrones is an epic tale of power, betrayal, and survival set in the fictional continents of Westeros and Essos.

Noble families wage war to claim the Iron Throne of the Seven Kingdoms, while an ancient supernatural threat rises beyond the great Wall of ice in the north — and a exiled princess across the sea builds an army to reclaim her family's legacy.

Created byDavid Benioff & D.B. Weiss

The Great Houses

Blood & Lineage

House Stark

"Winter is Coming"

Wardens of the North, ruling from Winterfell for thousands of years. Their direwolf sigil is feared across Westeros.

House Lannister

"Hear Me Roar"

The wealthiest house in Westeros, ruling from Casterly Rock. Their gold funds kingdoms and their cunning topples thrones.

House Targaryen

"Fire and Blood"

Ancient dragonlords of Valyria who conquered Westeros three centuries ago. Their bond with dragons is unmatched in history.
